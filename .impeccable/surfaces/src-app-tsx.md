# Surface: Manor Casebook app (quiz → results)

Mode: Operate (the player completes a questionnaire and reads a recommendation), with an Experience moment at the result reveal.
Audience/job: Identity V players (phone and PC equally) finding which survivor(s) to play and why.
Constraints: three user-selectable themes (IDV gothic-Victorian, clean modern dark, playful/cute) share one structure; EN/中文 toggle; skins never affect ranking.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The recommendation is a Victorian manor playbill: the player's answers are the "Acts" of the evening, and the result bills survivors by typographic scale — the headliner's name set enormous, the supporting pool billed smaller in proportion to fit. Refuses the category default of a card grid of equal-weight results with percentage bars.

OWN-WORLD: Letterpress playbill grammar — wood-type condensed display caps, stacked centred billing lines, ornament rules and fleurons, two-ink printing (each theme commits to one ground + one ink + one accent). Gothic: soot-black ground, oxblood + tarnished brass inks. Modern: graphite ground, cool bone ink, single signal accent, no ornament. Playful: circus-bill saturated field (candy stripe band), chunky rounded display.

STORY: Player understands the quiz is a performance in acts they can shorten (Quick) or extend (Deep); believes the billing because every name carries the lines that earned it; acts by expanding a billed survivor to see why, matchups, learning path, skins, and exporting the bill as an image.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Masthead "MANOR CASEBOOK · 庄园档案" as a bill header; below, the evening's programme: Act list as a single unbroken progress row with a moving light marking "now"; primary action "Begin Act I" set as the largest line after the title; theme/language switches as small bill footnotes top-right.

FORM: Victorian circus/theatre playbill, position 5 on my ordered list (casebook, selection screen, cipher machine, evidence board, playbill, manor blueprint, tarot). Seed key 9263951c. Raises: from variety-telop, scale mapped strictly to intensity (fit %), never decoration; from drum-machine step row, the act row never wraps and a chase light shows where "now" is; from data-sublime, total two-ink commitment per theme.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
